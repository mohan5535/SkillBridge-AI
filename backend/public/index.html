About SkillBridge AI

Universal Career Gap Intelligence, Objective Re-Entry Science & Platform Mission

Our Mission

Eliminating Career Break Penalty Through Verified Skill Science

Millions of qualified professionals step away from the workforce each year due to caregiving, medical recovery, parental responsibilities, or educational pursuits. Traditional applicant tracking systems unfairly penalize chronological employment gaps, discarding seasoned talent. SkillBridge AI introduces a mathematical, evidence-grounded framework to audit transferable competencies, quantify actual readiness, and curate fast-track learning sequences to bridge validated gaps.

The Career Gap Problem

Employment hiatuses are frequently mischaracterized as skill depreciation. In reality, modern professionals in transition frequently acquire advanced management, technical, or domain capabilities during career intervals.

  • Chronological resume filters reject qualified candidates automatically
  • Zero distinction made between skill decay and transferable competency
  • Lack of structured roadmaps for targeted upskilling
  • Counselors and recruiters lack verified cohort analytics

The SkillBridge Solution

SkillBridge AI replaces resume keyword guessing with multi-factor relational telemetry.

  • Standardized, neutral career break logging under FR-02 protocol
  • Mathematical 5-factor weighted career readiness scoring
  • Query 8 exact skills comparison against industry role taxonomies
  • Graph-sequenced learning plans with verified assessment gates
